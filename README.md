## Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 Jun. 2026

Smoking Scripts (in the Discord server) collects limited data in order to maintain purchase records and improve performance.

### Information Collected

The following information may be collected in the server:

* Discord user IDs
* Discord usernames, display names, and user IDs
* Discord channel names and IDs
* Messages (and their metadata) sent within channels

### Information Not Collected

This script does not intentionally collect or store:

* Raw IP addresses
* Passwords
* Email addresses
* Payment information
* Personally identifying real-world information
* Digital items in possession

### Purpose of Data Collection

Collected information is used solely for purposes such as:

* Maintaining sales records to prevent fraud or abuse
* Data recovery
* Creating and managing private channels
* Diagnosing technical issues
* Generating responses from AI

### Data Retention

Channel transcripts are permanently retained; metadata is automatically anonymized to remove message sender information after 30 days.

### Data Sharing

Collected data is not sold to third parties. Aggregate or anonymized statistics are only used internally for analytics or development purposes.

### Security

Reasonable efforts are made to limit collected data and store it securely. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure.

### Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of the software after changes are made constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
